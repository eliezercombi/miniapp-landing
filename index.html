Features

Light, dark or a custom theme: the app picks up the colours you already use, so nothing looks out of place.

One button to pay

The big button at the bottom is Telegram's own. No forms, no card numbers typed into a web page.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It is a Mini App, so it opens inside Telegram on your phone or computer. If you have Telegram, you already have it.

How do I pay?

With Telegram Stars, TON, or a card through Telegram's payment screen. You can also choose to pay at pickup.

Is my Telegram account safe?

The app never sees your phone number or your messages. Telegram only shares your name and a signed proof that the request really comes from you.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, until it is marked "in progress". Reply to the order message in the chat and it is done.

Skip the queue tomorrow morning.

Open the menu now and save your usual. It takes less than a minute.
